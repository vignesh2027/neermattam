How the measurement works

{{template "wheel-diagram"}}
  1. Read the scene. Muse Spark finds the water and a reference of known size, usually a wheel.
  2. Measure. SAM masks the wheel and the water, and Muse Spark checks every mask before it is used. Where they meet is the waterline; the hidden part of the wheel is the depth. With no measurable wheel, you get Muse Spark's own estimate, clearly labelled.
  3. Check and fuse. The report is checked for freshness, agreement with nearby cells and recent rain, then fused into the map with its uncertainty.
